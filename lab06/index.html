Lab 05: Best Design Practices

During this reflection, I began to comprehend the reason effective design methods are essential while developing a webpage. A webpage ought to be easy to explore, well arranged, and simple to comprehend. Clear and easy to read fonts, sufficient space among the elements, matching colors can cause a webpage to appear better. I will love to keep on improving these concepts in order that I will be able to create website pages that are equally appealing and simple to access. Additionally, I have to think about ease of access in order to have various individuals make use of the website easily. I made use of the assignment instructions as well and also the lab examples to guide me.

Important Design Practices

  • Use the right and easy to read fonts and font sizes.
  • Keep the layout well-arranged and organized.
  • Make the navigation vivid and not complex to utilize.
  • Test the page on a desktop screens or on a phones.
